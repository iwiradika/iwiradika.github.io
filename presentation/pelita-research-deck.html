COGNITIVE DIAGNOSTIC RESEARCH · 2026

Profiling Digital Competency of Prospective Vocational IT Educators

A cognitive diagnostic approach using the Generalized DINA (G-DINA) model to move from a single score to attribute-level mastery profiles.

BY
I Nyoman Indhi Wiradika
01THE PROBLEM

Why diagnose, not just score?

Indonesia faces a shortage of digitally competent vocational teachers, yet most instruments report a single aggregate score that cannot reveal which specific competencies are missing.

A cognitive diagnostic model turns one number into a mastery profile teacher-education programs can act on.

02THE MODEL

Four attributes, mapped by the G-DINA Q-matrix

The G-DINA model estimates the probability that a test-taker has mastered each latent attribute required to answer an item, producing a binary mastered / not-mastered profile across four competency dimensions.

KTI
IT Content Knowledge
Using, developing and managing ICT for educational purposes.
PD
Digital Pedagogy
Designing and evaluating learning that integrates digital technology.
PRO
Digital Professionalism
Ethical, reflective and professional conduct with digital technology.
KSD
Digital Social Engagement
Building socio-professional relationships through digital technology.

08RESULTS · PROFILES

What the profiles revealed

Diagnostic profiling surfaced patterns a single score would have hidden, with direct implications for curriculum design.

GENDER
Female students outperformed males
consistently, across all four dimensions, countering proficiency stereotypes.
TRAJECTORY
A non-linear dip in semester 3
a significant decline followed by recovery, a critical transition point for support.
DOMAINS
Digital Pedagogy strongest; IT Content & Social Engagement variable
pinpointing exactly where curricular attention should go.
09CONCLUSION

A robust diagnostic tool for targeted teacher preparation

The G-DINA-based instrument reliably identifies specific learning needs, enabling programs to intervene during critical transition periods instead of reacting to a single final grade.
