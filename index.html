02

Research

The common thread is measurement that teachers and policymakers can act on: tests that say what a student can and cannot do yet, that treat groups of students fairly, and that hold up when new tools such as language models enter the process.

Cognitive diagnostic models

Reporting which skills a student has mastered, not only a total score. Current work applies the G-DINA model to the digital competency of future vocational IT teachers.

Read: Understanding CDMs →

Test fairness

Checking whether items work the same way for groups of students with equal ability, using differential item functioning.

Read: Is this test fair? →

LLMs as measurement tools

Asking when scores produced by a language model can be trusted, and why a consistent model is not automatically a correct one.

Read: Consistent is not correct →

Large-scale assessment

Reading international results such as PISA for what they say about Indonesian students and classrooms.

Read: Behind the PISA numbers →

03

Lab

Assessment built on evidence, not assumptions.

AssevLabs is Wiradika’s research-based assessment lab. It moves measurement methods from research into tools that schools, researchers and organizations use to design instruments, run assessments and report results they can trace and defend.

  • Instrument design

    Items, competencies, attributes and Q-matrices, with expert review of content, language and quality.

  • Item banks

    Item banks and assessment packages for schools, research projects and competency evaluation in organizations.

  • Expert review

    A traceable record of how each instrument was reviewed and improved.

  • Measurement analysis

    Scoring and analysis with classical test theory, item response theory and cognitive diagnostic models.
